# Portal Features, Multilingual Access, and Photo Evidence

InsightGov provides accessible, modern GovTech tools designed for all citizens across Tamil Nadu:

### Multilingual Support
- The portal interface is fully localized in English, Tamil (தமிழ்), and Hindi (हिन्दी).
- Citizens can submit petitions and interact with the AI assistant in their preferred language. The system transparently interprets vernacular descriptions and routes them to appropriate departments.

### Voice-to-Text Dictation
- Citizens who prefer speaking over typing can use the microphone button in the petition form to dictate their grievance naturally using browser speech recognition.

### Geographic Map Pinning
- Accurate location coordinates are critical for municipal field workers. Citizens can either use "Detect My Location" or drag a pin on the interactive Leaflet map to pinpoint the exact issue spot.
- The system checks device location integrity and applies a 200-meter radius calculation to identify duplicate issues.

### Photographic Evidence
- Citizens can upload up to 5 photos showing physical evidence (e.g. broken pipe, road crater, exposed wires).
- InsightGov analyzes image relevance and records visual evidence in the petition record to assist field officers during site visits.

# How to Submit a Petition

Submitting a petition on InsightGov is simple and transparent:

1. Log in to your Citizen Dashboard.
2. Click the "Submit Petition" button.
3. Fill out the title and description clearly. Include any relevant details like location, dates, or involved parties.
4. (Optional) Provide location details for map-based analysis.
5. Click "Submit".

**What happens next?**
Your petition is immediately analyzed by our secure, local AI system. The AI will automatically read your petition, categorize it, detect if it's a duplicate of an existing petition, assign a priority level, and route it to the correct government department. You do not need to select the department yourself!

You will receive a unique tracking ID to monitor your petition's status.

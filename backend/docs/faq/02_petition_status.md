# Understanding Petition Statuses

Your petition will move through several statuses on InsightGov:

* **Pending**: Your petition has been received and is waiting for the AI to analyze it. This usually takes just a few seconds.
* **Analyzed**: The AI has completed its analysis (categorization, priority prediction, department routing, and duplicate detection). It is now waiting for a government officer to review it.
* **Under Review**: A human government officer in the assigned department is currently reviewing your petition.
* **Resolved**: The issue has been addressed and the petition is closed.
* **Rejected**: The petition was deemed invalid, non-actionable, or lacking required details.

You can check your petition's status at any time in the Citizen Dashboard or by asking this chat assistant.

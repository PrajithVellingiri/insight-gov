# Citizen Rights, Tracking, and Resolution Verification

InsightGov ensures complete accountability between citizens and departmental officers throughout the grievance lifecycle:

### Petition Lifecycle States
1. **Pending**: The petition has been submitted by the citizen and is queued for AI analysis and department assignment.
2. **Analysed**: The AI system has extracted key facts, assigned a priority tier, predicted the handling department, and checked for geographic duplicates.
3. **Under Review**: An assigned departmental officer has taken ownership of the petition and is conducting field inspection or remedial work.
4. **Resolved**: Work has been completed. Officers are mandated to upload photographic proof of resolution before marking any grievance as resolved.
5. **Rejected**: If the petition is found invalid, outside state jurisdiction, or a duplicate, with reasons formally documented by the officer.
6. **Withdrawn**: Citizens can withdraw their petition at any time while the status remains "pending" or "analysed".

### Mandatory Resolution Proof
To prevent premature closure of civic grievances, the InsightGov system strictly requires departmental officers to upload a verified "Resolution Proof Image" and written closing remarks before the status can transition to "Resolved".

### Citizen Notifications
Citizens receive in-app real-time alerts whenever their petition advances to a new status stage, when transferred to another department, or when marked resolved.

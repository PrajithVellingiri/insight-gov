# Grievance Redressal Timelines and Priority Levels

InsightGov categorizes grievances into four distinct priority tiers to ensure urgent issues receive immediate governmental attention:

### Priority Levels and Standards

1. **Critical Priority**
   - Criteria: Immediate hazard to human life or public safety, active contamination of drinking water sources, exposed high-voltage cables, or collapsed bridge/roadway cutoffs.
   - Initial Action Target: Within 24 to 48 hours of assignment.
   - Review: Monitored at senior departmental officer levels.

2. **High Priority**
   - Criteria: Major civic disruptions affecting entire neighbourhoods, overflowing sewage mains, non-functioning streetlights on major thoroughfares, or water supply interruptions exceeding 48 hours.
   - Action Target: Resolution targeted within 3 to 7 working days.

3. **Medium Priority**
   - Criteria: Moderate community inconvenience, localized road potholes, damaged garbage collection bins, or pending routine municipal maintenance.
   - Action Target: Resolution targeted within 7 to 14 working days.

4. **Low Priority**
   - Criteria: Minor aesthetic repairs, individual inquiries, or long-term civic planning suggestions.
   - Action Target: Resolution targeted within 15 to 30 working days.

### Priority Escalation by Community Impact
If multiple independent citizens submit duplicate reports for the same issue within a 200-meter radius, InsightGov automatically escalates the priority level (e.g. from Medium to High, or High to Critical) to reflect acute community concern.

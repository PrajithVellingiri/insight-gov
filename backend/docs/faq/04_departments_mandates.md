# Government Department Mandates and Responsibilities

InsightGov routes citizen petitions across official Government of Tamil Nadu departments based on their designated mandates:

### Municipal Administration and Water Supply Department (MAWS)
- Handles urban civic infrastructure in city corporations, municipalities, and town panchayats.
- Key responsibilities: Drinking water supply pipelines, underground drainage and sewage systems, street sweeping, municipal garbage disposal, street lighting maintenance in urban areas, and local park upkeep.

### Public Works Department (PWD)
- Oversees construction, structural maintenance, and architectural upkeep of state government buildings, court complexes, and administrative offices.

### Highways and Minor Ports Department
- Responsible for state highways, major district roads, bypasses, flyovers, road bridges, culverts, and minor sea landing ports. Handles pothole repair, road resurfacing, and traffic barriers on state highway routes.

### Water Resources Department (WRD)
- Manages major dams, reservoirs, river basins, irrigation canals, and flood control embankments. Handles desilting of irrigation channels and agricultural water distribution.

### Energy Department (TANGEDCO / TANTRANSCO)
- Regulates electrical power generation, transmission, and local distribution. Handles electrical transformers, power outages, damaged electric poles, overhead cable safety, and low voltage issues.

### Revenue and Disaster Management Department
- Administers land administration, patta/chitta records, land boundary disputes, certificates (community, income, nativity), relief for natural calamities (floods, cyclones, droughts), and stamp duties.

### Health and Family Welfare Department
- Operates government medical college hospitals, district headquarters hospitals, primary health centres (PHCs), ambulance services (108), and public vaccination programmes.

### School Education Department
- Administers government and aided primary, middle, and higher secondary schools. Oversees teacher welfare, mid-day meal implementation, and free school textbook distribution.

### Rural Development and Panchayat Raj Department
- Manages village infrastructure, rural link roads, village water supply, panchayat sanitation, and the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme (MGNREGS).

### Transport Department
- Coordinates government state transport corporation buses (TNSTC), regional transport offices (RTO), vehicle fitness certificates, and driving license administration.

### Environment, Climate Change and Forests Department
- Manages reserved forests, wildlife sanctuaries, state afforestation, and pollution control standards via the Tamil Nadu Pollution Control Board (TNPCB).

### Mudalvarin Mugavari Department
- Serves as the Chief Minister's Special Grievance Redressal mechanism, monitoring inter-departmental issues and complex grievances requiring executive oversight.
